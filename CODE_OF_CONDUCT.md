# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful, constructive, precise about evidence, and generous in technical disagreement. Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate privacy violations, and publishing another person's trace without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact described in `SECURITY.md`; reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. This text is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
